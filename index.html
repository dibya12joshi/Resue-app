Emergency Animal First Aid

Basic action plans to stabilize injured animals before rescue teams arrive.

🐶

Stray Dog First Aid

For injured or bleeding dogs:

  1. Assess safety: Ensure the dog is not aggressive. Offer a gentle voice.
  2. Stop bleeding: Apply gentle pressure using a clean cloth or bandage.
  3. Keep warm: Wrap in a soft towel or blanket to prevent shock.
  4. Report details: Take a photo and specify landmarks on our dashboard.
🐱

Cat Rescue Guide

For trapped or injured felines:

  1. Approach carefully: Cats scratch/bite when in pain. Use thick gloves if possible.
  2. Secure container: Place the cat gently in a cardboard box with air holes.
  3. Hydration: If dehydrated, offer water, but do not force feed.
  4. Minimize noise: Keep in a dark, quiet place until the volunteer arrives.
🐦

Bird Rehabilitation

For wings broken or bird exhaustion:

  1. Avoid cages: Place in a cardboard box lined with tissues or cloth.
  2. Handle gently: Scoop from behind, locking wings close to the body.
  3. Warmth is key: Keep the box in a warm location.
  4. Report: File a Bird alert immediately for medical volunteer pickup.
🦌

Wildlife & Large Animals

For wild birds, snakes, or cattle:

  1. Do NOT touch: Wildlife requires professional wildlife handlers.
  2. Keep distance: Secure the perimeter to prevent the animal from fleeing.
  3. Photo detail: Note size, behavior, and landmarks.
  4. Call admin: Submit an urgent alert using the dashboard tag "Wildlife".
